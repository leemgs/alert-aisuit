\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\textbf{What drives the gains.} The gains come from knowing \emph{which} authority is still operative---the extracted, validated treatment layer---and from applying that knowledge before generation; the gate itself (Eq.~\eqref{eq:tpg_score}) is intentionally simple, and the agents and ingestion schema are supporting infrastructure. Table~\ref{tab:temporal_ablation} isolates the effect of applying the layer, and B5 rules out extra refinement passes, though not extra LLM calls, as the source of the gain.

\textbf{Construct validity.} Agreement of the treatment graph with a commercial citator is measured (P2, Appendix~\ref{app:p2}): negative-treatment micro-F1 0.81, 20.4\% missed closures, and a 3.8\% PLRE decision-flip rate after citator correction. Transfer to external retrieval stacks (P1, Appendix~\ref{app:p1}) is not yet established: a gold-closure pilot removed stale precedent on three hosts but met its pre-fixed criterion on only one.
