\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\textbf{What drives the gains.} The gains come from knowing \emph{which} authority is still operative---the extracted, validated treatment layer---and from applying that knowledge before generation; the gate itself (Eq.~\eqref{eq:tpg_score}) is intentionally simple, and the agents and ingestion schema are supporting infrastructure. Table~\ref{tab:temporal_ablation} isolates the effect of applying the layer, and the compute-matched B5 baseline shows the gain is not explained by extra LLM calls alone. The operator is general in form, but our evidence is confined to one domain (U.S.\ AI litigation) and one system; we do not yet claim that its gains transfer.

\textbf{Construct validity.} Agreement of the treatment graph with a commercial citator is measured (P2, Appendix~\ref{app:p2}): negative-treatment micro-F1 0.81, 20.4\% missed closures, and a 3.8\% PLRE decision-flip rate after citator correction. Transfer of the gate to external retrieval stacks (P1, Appendix~\ref{app:p1}) is specified in advance but not yet executed.
