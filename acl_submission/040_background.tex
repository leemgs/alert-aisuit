\section{Background and Scope}

\textbf{Legal-risk monitoring for AI products.} Copyright litigation against generative-AI systems has grown sharply since 2022, challenging web-scale training data~\citep{henderson2023foundation,levendowski2018copyright} under theories spanning DMCA \S1202 information removal, direct infringement, and vicarious liability for outputs~\citep{grimmelmann2015copyright}. Legal-operations teams track dockets, news, and internal product changes, but the bridge from a litigation event to a product-specific engineering action is usually manual. ALERT targets this product-facing layer.

\textbf{Why time matters.} Most legal NLP treats its corpus as a static collection. In litigation sensing, however, a precedent's relevance depends on whether it had been narrowed, vacated, superseded, or overruled at the relevant date. ALERT makes this validity condition explicit and exposes it to retrieval, entailment, and mitigation generation.

\textbf{Decision-support boundary.} ALERT emits risk intelligence, evidence summaries, and mitigation candidates with provenance and residual uncertainty for qualified reviewers; it does not provide legal advice, replace counsel, or predict outcomes (see Ethics Statement).
