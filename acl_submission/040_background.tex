\section{Background and Scope}

\textbf{Legal-risk monitoring for AI products.} Copyright litigation against generative-AI systems has grown sharply since 2022, challenging web-scale training data~\citep{henderson2023foundation,levendowski2018copyright} under theories spanning DMCA \S1202 information removal, direct infringement, and vicarious liability for outputs~\citep{grimmelmann2015copyright}. Legal-operations teams track dockets, news, and internal product changes, but the bridge from a litigation event to a product-specific engineering action is usually manual. ALERT targets this product-facing layer. Its decision-support boundary is set out in the Ethics Statement.
