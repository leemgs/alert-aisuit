\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\textbf{Legal NLP and entailment.} Legal NLP has studied judgment prediction~\citep{chalkidis2019neural}, statutory reasoning~\citep{holzenberger2020sara}, legal understanding benchmarks~\citep{chalkidis2022lexglue}, case retrieval and entailment~\citep{rabelo2022coliee}, and summarization~\citep{huang2021efficient,manor2019plain}, often with domain-adapted encoders~\citep{chalkidis2020legalbert,zheng2021legalbert,xiao2021lawformer} built on pretrained transformers~\citep{vaswani2017attention,devlin2019bert}. LegalBench evaluates legal reasoning~\citep{guha2023legalbench}, and audits find that LLMs and commercial legal RAG tools still hallucinate~\citep{dahl2024hallucinating,magesh2024hallucination}. These works take legal text as the premise, treat the corpus as static, and do not test whether retrieved authority was still operative at a given date. PLRE changes the inference unit: it maps product attributes to legal-risk theories, and the relation is directional, product-conditioned, and temporal.

\textbf{Time-sensitive QA and temporal retrieval.} A line of work shows that answers depend on when a question is asked: SituatedQA conditions QA on temporal and geographic context~\citep{zhang2021situatedqa}, TimeQA and StreamingQA evaluate time-sensitive and evolving knowledge~\citep{chen2021timeqa,liska2022streamingqa}, RealTime QA targets answers that change over time~\citep{kasai2023realtimeqa}, and time-aware LMs condition parametric knowledge on time~\citep{dhingra2022timeaware}; temporal KG QA reasons over time-stamped facts~\citep{saxena2021cronkgqa}, and temporal IR uses document time as a ranking signal~\citep{campos2014temporal}. These settings treat time as a property of \emph{facts} or \emph{queries}. In PLRE, time enters through \emph{supersession}: a later document changes the evidential status of an earlier one, so validity must be propagated along treatment edges rather than read from a timestamp.

\textbf{Citators and legal citation graphs.} Commercial citators such as Shepard's and KeyCite track whether authority has been overruled, questioned, or negatively treated~\citep{shepards2024,keycite2024}; we do not claim time-varying authority itself as new. Citation-network analysis measures the importance of precedent~\citep{fowler2007network}, and prior work labels the semantics of citation edges automatically~\citep{sadeghian2018semantic}. These efforts build or characterize the graph; ALERT instead attaches validity intervals to extracted treatment edges and applies them as a \emph{retrieval-time gate} (Eq.~\eqref{eq:tpg_score}, Fig.~\ref{fig:tpg_mech}) that feeds a downstream product-risk task.

\textbf{RAG, agents, and risk control.} RAG~\citep{lewis2020retrieval,karpukhin2020dense,gao2023retrieval} usually runs a fixed retrieval plan before generation, while agent frameworks~\citep{yao2022react,shinn2023reflexion,wei2022chain,langgraph2024,autogpt2023,park2023generative,wang2024voyager,wang2024multiagent} show gains from iterative tool use, but self-refinement stops when the model deems its answer satisfactory. ALERT ties stopping to an externally inspectable predicate over schema completeness, evidence support, and mitigation coverage, and uses conformal risk control~\citep{angelopoulos2023conformalrisk,angelopoulos2021ltt,geifman2017selective} narrowly for evidence omission, not for legal correctness.
