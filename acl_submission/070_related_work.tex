\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}

\textbf{Legal NLP and entailment.} Legal NLP has studied judgment prediction~\citep{chalkidis2019neural}, statutory reasoning~\citep{louis2023statutory}, legal understanding benchmarks~\citep{chalkidis2022lexglue}, case retrieval and entailment~\citep{rabelo2022coliee}, and summarization~\citep{huang2021efficient,manor2019plain}, often with domain-adapted encoders~\citep{chalkidis2020legalbert,zheng2021legalbert,xiao2021lawformer} built on pretrained transformers~\citep{vaswani2017attention,devlin2019bert}. These tasks take legal text as the premise and largely treat the corpus as static. PLRE changes the inference unit: it maps product attributes to legal-risk theories, and the relation is directional, product-conditioned, and temporal.

\textbf{Citators and time-aware retrieval.} Commercial citators such as Shepard's and KeyCite track whether authority has been overruled, questioned, or negatively treated~\citep{shepards2024,keycite2024}; we do not claim time-varying authority itself as new. Time-aware language models~\citep{dhingra2022timeaware} condition \emph{parametric} knowledge on time, whereas precedent-retrieval work typically treats the citation graph as static or encodes time only as a ranking feature, so a lexically close authority can still dominate retrieval after it has been narrowed or overruled. ALERT instead extracts a public-corpus treatment graph, attaches validity intervals, and applies them as a \emph{retrieval-time gate} (Eq.~\eqref{eq:tpg_score}, Fig.~\ref{fig:tpg_mech}) feeding a downstream product-risk task.

\textbf{RAG, agents, and risk control.} RAG~\citep{lewis2020retrieval,karpukhin2020dense,gao2023retrieval} usually runs a fixed retrieval plan before generation, while agent frameworks~\citep{yao2022react,shinn2023reflexion,wei2022chain,langgraph2024,autogpt2023,park2023generative,wang2024voyager,wang2024multiagent} show gains from iterative tool use; self-refinement, however, stops when the model judges its own answer satisfactory. ALERT ties stopping to an externally inspectable predicate over schema completeness, evidence support, and mitigation coverage, and uses conformal risk control~\citep{angelopoulos2023conformalrisk,angelopoulos2021ltt,geifman2017selective} narrowly for evidence omission, not for legal correctness.
