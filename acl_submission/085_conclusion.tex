\section{Conclusion}

We introduced PLRE, a directional, time-conditioned entailment task linking AI-product attributes to legal-risk theories, and ALERT, whose extracted, citator-validated treatment layer tells retrieval which authority was operative at the query date. Validity gating improves PLRE F1 and sharply reduces stale citations over flat similarity, and the full system improves triage agreement over compute-matched baselines; open tests are listed under Limitations.
