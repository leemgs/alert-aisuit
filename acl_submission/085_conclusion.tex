\section{Conclusion}

We introduced PLRE, a directional, time-conditioned entailment task linking AI-product attributes to legal-risk theories, and ALERT, whose extracted, citator-validated treatment layer tells retrieval which authority was operative at the query date. Validity gating improves PLRE F1 and cuts stale citations relative to flat similarity. Open problems include raising negative-treatment recall, modeling partial treatments, and testing the gate on stronger external retrievers and other domains where later documents supersede earlier ones (e.g., regulations or retracted findings); the released testbed (Appendix~\ref{app:p1}) supports the first and third.
