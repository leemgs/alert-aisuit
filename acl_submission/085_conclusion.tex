\section{Conclusion}

We introduced PLRE, a directional, time-conditioned entailment task linking AI-product attributes to legal-risk theories, and ALERT, a system whose central component is a point-in-time authority gate that conditions retrieval on whether legal authority was valid at the query date, before evidence reaches the generator. Validity gating improves PLRE F1 and sharply reduces stale citations over flat similarity, and the full system improves triage agreement and report quality over compute-matched baselines, within a decision-support boundary. Open problems include higher treatment-edge recall, reducing reliance on retained pre-labels, external-stack transfer of the gate (P1), and broader PLRE coverage across product archetypes and jurisdictions.
