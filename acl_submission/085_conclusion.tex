\section{Conclusion}

We introduced PLRE, a directional, time-conditioned entailment task linking AI-product attributes to legal-risk theories, and ALERT, whose central component is an extracted, citator-validated treatment layer that tells retrieval which authority was still operative at the query date, before evidence reaches the generator. Validity gating improves PLRE F1 and sharply reduces stale citations over flat similarity, and the full system improves triage agreement over compute-matched baselines. Its main open tests---external-stack transfer (P1) and larger, more fully reported PLRE evaluation---are listed under Limitations.
