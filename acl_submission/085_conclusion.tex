\section{Conclusion}

We introduced PLRE, a directional, time-conditioned entailment task linking AI-product attributes to legal-risk theories, and ALERT, whose extracted, citator-validated treatment layer tells retrieval which authority was operative at the query date. Validity gating improves PLRE F1 and cuts stale citations relative to flat similarity. Open problems include raising negative-treatment recall, modeling partial treatments, and testing the gate on stronger retrievers and other domains with supersession (e.g., regulations); our released testbed (Appendix~\ref{app:p1}) supports this work.
