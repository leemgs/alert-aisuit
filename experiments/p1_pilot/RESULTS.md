# P1 pilot: results

Pilot of Protocol P1 (paper App. I.1) on public U.S. Supreme Court data,
**gold-treatment variant only**. Design frozen before any retrieval run at commit
`08aea76f2e923a1cc2d4828b39628d0e75d85407` (`DESIGN.md`). This is a *pilot of
slice (b)*, not P1: it must not be reported as "P1 executed".

Full tables: `RESULTS_TABLES.md` (generated by `report.py` from `results.json`).

## Verdict (pre-specified criterion, DESIGN.md §7)

**The pilot does not support transfer.** Only **1 of 3** hosts (bge) meets every
condition (ΔnDCG@10 > 0 and Δstale@10 < 0, both Holm p < 0.05, and
non-inferiority on controls). The criterion needs 2 of 3.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BM25 | 28.88 | 8.3% / 26.8% | +0.09 [−0.58, +0.52], 1.00 | −8.3 [−10.7, −6.2] pts, <0.0001 | +0.089 [+0.035, +0.162] | not met (nDCG) |
| bge-small | 8.35 | 5.5% / 17.5% | +0.13 [+0.04, +0.27], <0.0001 | −5.5 [−7.1, −3.9] pts, <0.0001 | +0.020 [+0.000, +0.049] | **met** |
| LegalBERT | 2.30 | 0.4% / 4.8% | +0.00 [+0.00, +0.00], 1.00 | −0.4 [−0.8, −0.1] pts, 0.034 | +0.000 [+0.000, +0.000] | not met (nDCG) |

Post-closure queries of full overrulings: 984 in 208 pair clusters. CIs: cluster
bootstrap by gold pair, 10,000 resamples. Sanity check (the gate never removes A
on pre-closure queries): **0 violations**.

## What the results say

1. **Q1, prevalence.** Standard retrievers do surface already-overruled precedent:
   BM25 ranks the overruled case in the top 10 for 8.3% of post-closure queries,
   bge for 5.5%. LegalBERT, which is a weak host here (nDCG@10 2.3; mean-pooled
   MLM encoder, first 512 tokens), almost never retrieves it (0.4%).
2. **Stale citations.** With gold intervals, the gate removes every overruled case
   from the top 10 on all three hosts (significant after Holm on all three).
3. **Retrieval quality.** The nDCG@10 gains are small (≤ 0.13 points as
   pre-specified) because removing documents can only help when the stale case
   ranks above the gold case. With gold intervals this is close to an upper bound
   (DESIGN.md §8). The effect is significant for bge only.
4. **No harm on controls.** Non-inferiority (margin −0.5 points) holds on all three
   hosts.
5. **Treatment weight.** The partial-overruling sweep gives identical rankings for
   w ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 0.75}. Partially overruled documents are rare in the top 10
   (33/149 queries for BM25, 17 for bge, 1 for LegalBERT), and any w < 1 in this
   range orders them the same way. The pilot is therefore uninformative about
   ALERT's `w(·)`, which is not specified in the paper or code.

## Post-hoc sensitivity analysis (NOT pre-specified; does not change the verdict)

Written after the primary results were seen (`sensitivity.py`, `sensitivity.json`).
BM25's negative tail comes from four post-closure queries (qids 1514, 1515, 1516,
1518; 1970–1987) whose gold case, *United States v. Rabinowitz* (339 U.S. 56,
1950), was itself overruled by *Chimel v. California* (1969). The gate correctly
removes an already-overruled gold case, so these gold labels conflict with the
design's own rule that authority closed before t_q is non-relevant. Excluding
them (n = 980):

| Host | ΔnDCG@10 [95% CI], p_Holm | Δstale@10 [95% CI], p_Holm |
|---|---|---|
| BM25 | +0.37 [+0.19, +0.57], <0.0001 | −8.4 [−10.8, −6.2] pts, <0.0001 |
| bge | +0.13 [+0.04, +0.27], <0.0001 | −5.5 [−7.1, −4.0] pts, <0.0001 |
| LegalBERT | +0.00 [+0.00, +0.00], 0.72 | −0.4 [−0.8, −0.1] pts, 0.034 |

Under this post-hoc exclusion 2 of 3 hosts would meet the criterion. Because the
exclusion was chosen after seeing results, it is reported only as a sensitivity
analysis. The pre-specified verdict stands.

## Deviations from the frozen design (all of them)

1. **CAP coverage ends in 2014.** 14 table entries whose overruling decision is
   not in CAP were excluded, plus 4 overrulings on or after 2020 (pre-specified)
   and 11 entries whose opinion text is under 1,000 characters. 253 of 282 parsed
   pairs remain (34 partial). Exclusions: `data/pairs_excluded.csv`.
2. **Corpus restricted** to opinions with ≥ 1,000 characters of text (29,030 of
   329,995 CAP U.S. Reports records).
3. **Post-closure queries** cite B on any point. The design asked for the point on
   which B overruled A, which cannot be identified automatically. Passages that
   mention A, its parties, or overruling language are dropped as designed.
4. **Pre-closure window** was tightened to end at A's *first* full overruling (not
   only this pair's B). This fixed 4 sanity-check violations from A cases with
   earlier overrulings (e.g. *Evans v. Gore*). It was made before any metric was
   looked at, and the query count went from 3,329 to 3,327.
5. **Candidate set** for each query: documents decided strictly before t_q,
   excluding the citing opinion itself (not stated in the design).
6. **BM25** via scikit-learn sparse matrices (k1 = 0.9, b = 0.4, Pyserini's
   defaults) rather than Pyserini.
7. **Dense hosts on CPU with truncation**, fixed before results were seen: bge uses
   the first 2 × 512-token chunks of each opinion, LegalBERT the first 512 tokens
   (max-chunk scoring as designed). This weakens both, LegalBERT severely.
8. **Treatment weight:** ALERT's `w(·)` is unavailable, so the pre-specified sweep
   was run. The indicator is implemented as removal from the ranked list, which is
   equivalent to multiplying by 0.
9. **Control queries** form their own clusters, one per query (they have no pair).
10. **Extractor-treatment variant not run.** The released code
    (`aigovsensing/ai-suit-sensing`) contains no treatment extractor.
11. **Passage rule:** sentence ±1 with abbreviation protection, and queries need
    ≥ 8 words after masking.
12. **bge model:** `BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5` (CLS pooling, query instruction
    prefix) as the open bge encoder.
13. **Query count** (3,327: pre 1,061, post 1,133, control 1,133) exceeds the
    design's target of about 500–750 plus controls. The per-pair cap of 5 was kept.
